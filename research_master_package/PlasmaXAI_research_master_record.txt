PlasmaXAI Research Master Record
======================================================================

1. Project identity
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Final preferred project name:
PlasmaXAI

Earlier intermediate naming variants that still appear in files:
- PlasmaXNet
- PLASMAXNET

Project purpose:
PlasmaXAI is an explainable AI framework for malignant plasma cell
recognition in multiple myeloma microscopy images. The project evolved
from a notebook-based benchmark into an optimized hybrid system and
finally into a counterfactual-guided learned fusion framework with
patient-level morphologic consistency analysis, multiple report
packages, architecture diagrams, and a doctor-facing website.

- Diseased patients showed a reproducible counterfactual signature centered on mean_r, mean_g, and mean_b.

16. Final concise conclusion
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The research progressed through three meaningful stages:
1. Initial notebook experimentation and baseline benchmarking
2. A stronger optimized hybrid ensemble
3. The final PlasmaXAI counterfactual-guided learned fusion framework

The final preferred system is PlasmaXAI at threshold 0.72, with:
- 93.42% accuracy
- 93.43% weighted F1
- 91.24% plasma precision
- 94.63% plasma recall
- 97.76% AUC

This is the main set of numbers to use for the final framework unless a
different operating point is explicitly needed for another presentation.

Final caution for reporting:
The cell-level classification results are strong and well supported.
The patient-level findings are promising and statistically supported
internally, but they remain exploratory and should be described
carefully until external validation is completed.
